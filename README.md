## What I've been building

**🚌 VRG Transport** — *NestJS · Next.js · MongoDB · Redis*
A university transport management system currently being rolled out with a city hall, replacing a fully manual 20-year-old process. Built with **hexagonal architecture**, it handles online enrollment, a **dynamic-priority waitlist**, and automatic ID card issuance with **real-time QR-code verification**. Includes cron jobs, SSE, transactional email, and legal accessibility compliance (WCAG).

**💠 Azo Conecta** — *Live at [azo.app.br](https://azo.app.br)*
A SaaS platform connecting professionals, businesses, and opportunities: online scheduling marketplace, business management tools, event module with real sign-ups, and a virtual card feature — with **Asaas** payment gateway and **Google API** integrations.

**🎬 Video Management Platform** — *NestJS · Next.js · MongoDB*
A client portal for a video-production business: credit purchases, uploads, and real-time process tracking. Integrates the **TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube** APIs to collect metrics and build analytics dashboards, with OAuth across multiple providers.

> Most source code lives in private repositories. Happy to walk through architecture and decisions on request.
